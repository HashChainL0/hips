## Abstract

Two or three plain sentences. What does this proposal do?

## Motivation

What problem does it solve, and for whom? Why is the current design not enough?

## Specification

The proposed design, precise enough that another team could build it. Use MUST, SHOULD and MAY in capitals where normative language is needed. Mark anything undecided as "to be specified". Label any worked example as an example, and do not invent figures.

## Rationale

Why this design and not the alternatives? Record the options that were considered and rejected.

## Security considerations

What can go wrong? Cover privacy loss, loss of funds, abuse and trust assumptions. A HIP without this section is not complete. Informational and Meta HIPs may write "none" with a reason.
